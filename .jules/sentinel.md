## 2024-03-21 - [CRITICAL] Unsafe Math and Borrows in Solana Account Utils
**Vulnerability:** Found direct usage of `+=` and `-=` for modifying account lamports, as well as `.borrow_mut()` for lamports and data in `programs/mpl-core/src/utils/account.rs`. Modifying lamports using unchecked math can lead to integer overflow/underflow panics. Using `.borrow_mut()` can cause ungraceful program panics (double borrows).
**Learning:** Solana programs require defensive math operations (`checked_add`, `checked_sub`) and safe borrowing (`try_borrow_mut_lamports`, `try_borrow_mut_data`) when managing lamports and account data directly. Unsafe arithmetic on lamports or unwrapping options/results can cause abrupt panics.
**Prevention:** Always strictly use `.checked_add()` and `.checked_sub()` for lamport modifications, handling errors securely (e.g., returning `MplCoreError::NumericalOverflowError`). Replace `.borrow_mut()` with `.try_borrow_mut_lamports()?` and `.try_borrow_mut_data()?`.
