## 2024-05-18 - Missing Checks on Arithmetic and Direct Dereferencing of `borrow_mut()`
**Vulnerability:** Double Borrow runtime panics caused by ungraceful `.borrow_mut()` on unaliased/aliased RefCells, and silent numerical underflow/overflow panics via unchecked `+=`/`-=`.
**Learning:** In Solana smart contracts handling `AccountInfo`, explicitly replace `.borrow_mut()` with the checked wrapper `try_borrow_mut_lamports()?` to ensure transactions gracefully fail with clean errors instead of panicking on double-borrows (critical for composed actions updating multiple accounts, like collecting fees). Always map arithmetic ops on system accounts/lamports with `.checked_add` and `.checked_sub` to `.ok_or(MplCoreError::NumericalOverflowError)?`.
**Prevention:** Avoid `+=` and `-=` entirely in Solana program crates. Always use `checked_sub`/`checked_add`. Prevent panic side effects from ungraceful `.borrow_mut()` by using `try_borrow_mut_lamports()?` in `AccountInfo` updates.
