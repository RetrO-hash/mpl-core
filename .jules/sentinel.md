## 2024-05-31 - [High] Fix RefCell panics and ungraceful panics when modifying lamports
**Vulnerability:** Widespread use of `.borrow_mut()` on AccountInfo lamports/data and `+=`/`-=` operators for lamport transfers in core utilities and processors.
**Learning:** Solana AccountInfo fields use `Rc<RefCell>`, meaning `.borrow_mut()` can cause unexpected runtime panics if a double-borrow occurs. These panics can be weaponized by attackers to cause Denial of Service (DoS) or force transactions to fail under specific conditions. Furthermore, unchecked arithmetic (`+=` and `-=`) on lamport balances leaves the contract vulnerable to integer overflow/underflow attacks.
**Prevention:** Always use `.try_borrow_mut_lamports()?` and `.try_borrow_mut_data()?` which bubble up a safe `ProgramError` instead of panicking. Always use `.checked_add()` and `.checked_sub()` coupled with `.ok_or(MplCoreError::NumericalOverflowError)?` for modifying lamport values to prevent overflow panics.
