## 2024-05-24 - [CRITICAL] Unsafe Math and Unhandled RefCell Borrows in Lamport Transfers
**Vulnerability:** Found multiple instances where lamport transfers used unsafe math operators (`+=`, `-=`) and mixed unsafe mutable borrows (`borrow_mut()`) instead of checking for overlaps. Additionally, the fallback value for writing Uninitialized data to closed accounts was unsafely fetched using `.unwrap()`. This could lead to runtime program panics, acting as a potential DoS attack vector.
**Learning:** Mixing standard math (`+=`) instead of `checked_add`/`checked_sub` and direct `.borrow_mut()` instead of `.try_borrow_mut_lamports()` within account reallocation methods bypasses graceful error handling, reverting the entire transaction via panic without detailed custom error traces.
**Prevention:** Strictly enforce the use of `try_borrow_mut_lamports()`, `try_borrow_mut_data()`, and `checked_add/sub()` mapped to specific contract errors instead of `.unwrap()` and standard arithmetic. Always separate the read of a value and the mutable borrow of the target into distinct, sequential statements to prevent RefCell "already borrowed" panics.
