## 2025-01-20 - [Fix Double Borrow and Overflow Panics in Solana Lamports Manipulation]
**Vulnerability:** Found unhandled integer arithmetic operators (`+=`, `-=`) and direct `lamports.borrow_mut()` on RefCells, leading to potential panics on double borrow or arithmetic overflow in Rust programs handling Solana lamports.
**Learning:** Using `+=` and `-=` is unsafe in Solana as arithmetic overflow or underflow would crash the program. Directly modifying `.lamports.borrow_mut()` causes ungraceful crashes during overlapping account processing, acting as an attack vector for Denial of Service via crafted instructions. In addition, when updating `try_borrow_mut_lamports()`, the new value must be computed *before* assigning it, otherwise Rust's assignment ordering evaluates the borrow before computing the value on the right-hand side, causing a double borrow panic anyway.
**Prevention:** Always use `.checked_add()` and `.checked_sub()` and map missing results to `NumericalOverflowError`. Refactor `.lamports.borrow_mut()` to `.try_borrow_mut_lamports()?`. Ensure left-hand and right-hand evaluations of an assignment statement do not cause overlapping accesses by extracting intermediate computations to independent lines before the assignment.
